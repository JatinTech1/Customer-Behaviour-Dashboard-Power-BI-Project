# 📊 Customer Behaviour Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Customer Behaviour Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze customer purchasing patterns, subscription behavior, revenue generation, and sales performance across different product categories and age groups.

This dashboard helps businesses gain actionable insights into customer behavior, identify high-performing product categories, and understand the contribution of various customer segments to overall revenue and sales.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Track overall business performance through KPIs.
- Compare revenue and sales across product categories.
- Understand customer subscription trends.
- Identify the most valuable customer age groups.
- Enable dynamic filtering for deeper analysis.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### ✅ Customer Subscription Trend
- Majority of customers (73%) are non-subscribers.
- Opportunity exists to improve customer retention through subscription programs.

### ✅ Best Performing Category
- Clothing is the highest revenue-generating category.
- It also records the highest number of sales transactions.

### ✅ Customer Segmentation
- Young Adults contribute the highest revenue and sales.
- Middle-aged customers are the second most valuable segment.

### ✅ Product Performance
- Accessories perform strongly after Clothing.
- Footwear and Outerwear show lower sales and revenue, indicating areas for business improvement.
